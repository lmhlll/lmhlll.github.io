Biography

I am currently a deep learning algorithm engineer at Baidu Inc.（百度）. Previously, I received my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology（哈工大）, supervised by Prof. Hongxun Yao. Before that, I got my Master's degree from Sun Yat-sen University（中山大学）. My research interests include image processing and computer vision, mainly focusing on few-shot learning.
